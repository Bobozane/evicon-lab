# Pilot Material Review

Stage 21.1 revises the original development-only pilot materials. It does not
change their provenance, split, or non-WVS status.

- Positive wording ceiling: each value dimension now has a balanced pair, with
  one reverse-scored item representing a reasonable competing consideration.
- Missing decision tradeoffs: each Chinese synthetic scenario now states two
  comparable actions and a conditional alternative, and asks for a provisional
  recommendation, uncertainty, and evidence that could change it.
- Cross-language interpretation: English probes and Chinese scenarios are
  documented as a cross-language engineering pilot only. Final research needs
  separately validated English core and Chinese applied arms.

The review does not establish construct validity, language equivalence, model
behavior, value change, conformity, or any causal effect. No material is a
frozen benchmark.
